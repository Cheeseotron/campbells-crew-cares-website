In their words

“CCC has provided so much support for myself as I navigated a new path to being a single mother to a child with special needs. At a time we needed it most, CCC was able to help us with diapers and wipes, clothes, a jacket, shoes and even a toy for Robbie.

They treated me with respect through the entire process, and even when I openly rejected the help—even though I desperately needed it—they insisted on providing these items to help me. I was struggling financially, and the relief I got knowing that Robbie had warm and properly fitting clothes greatly helped me and will never be forgotten.”

Mandi Dalton / CCC testimony
