Winter 2025 Kids Shopping Event.

On December 6, 2025, 100 children in need came together for a day of shopping, connection, and holiday care. Each child received $125 worth of clothing—including a shirt, pants, shoes, socks, underwear, and a jacket—along with books, stuffed animals, blankets, and goodie bags.

Past events

Years of showing up.

From seasonal clothing events to backpacks and school supplies, each gathering has brought volunteers and families together around practical care.

This archive shares many of our past events, but it is nowhere near a complete record. We are continually providing aid, and many smaller efforts are not heavily photographed. Those quiet acts of care mean every bit as much.

2,000 Food Bags
