Our work

Basic needs.
Lasting hope.

We bring people together to provide food, clothing, and essential supplies when children and families need support.

01 / Clothing

Clean clothes. Lasting confidence.

We are passionate about clothing kids so they feel confident, stay warm, and have clean clothes to wear every day. Every child deserves that comfort and dignity, and we strive to make it possible.

02 / Food

Everyone deserves to be fed.

Campbell knows firsthand that access to food can be complicated. Although he takes in plenty of food, his body absorbs only a small amount because of his medical conditions, which is why he relies on a feeding tube. The support others have shown him drives our belief that everyone deserves access to the nourishment their body needs.

03 / Essential supplies

The essentials of daily life.

Life throws curveballs. Whether insurance denies necessary medical supplies or a family cannot afford what their children need to return to school, we strive to provide the simple, essential items people need for daily life.

Know someone who needs help?

Tell us their story.

Reach out with the situation, the essential items needed, and the best way for our crew to follow up. When we are able to help, Campbell's Crew Cares provides basic necessities directly—such as food, clothing, school essentials, and certain medical supplies.

Please know: We do not provide cash, checks, gift cards, reimbursements, or currency in any form. We also do not provide nonessential items such as toys or vehicles. Requests must be for basic necessities needed in daily life.
