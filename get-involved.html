Helpful information

Common
questions.

Quick answers about receiving help, volunteering, partnering, and donating.

How do I nominate someone for help?

Use the email panel above and briefly explain the situation, the essential items needed, and the best way for our crew to follow up.

How can I volunteer?

Email us with your interests and availability. We’ll share upcoming opportunities to join an event or community project.

Can my organization partner with CCC?

Yes. We welcome conversations with businesses, schools, teams, and community organizations whose ideas fit our mission.

How can I donate?

Use any Donate button on the website to give securely through our PayPal donation page.
